# Privacy Policy for Reverse Play **Effective Date:** [November 20, 2026] **Developer:** Saad LAKHTYARI **Contact:** Slakhtyari@gmail.com This Privacy Policy explains how **Reverse Play** ("the App") handles information when you use our Android application. ## 1. On-Device Audio Processing & Storage Reverse Play requires access to your device's microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`) solely to record audio that you choose to reverse and play back. - **100% Local Processing:** All audio recording, reversal, and playback happen locally on your device. We do not upload, stream, transmit, or store your voice recordings on any external servers. - **Local Exports:** When you choose to export a reversed recording as a WAV file, the file is saved directly to your device's local storage via Android MediaStore under your control. ## 2. Information We Do Not Collect Reverse Play does not require account registration and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise GPS location. ## 3. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob) To keep Reverse Play free, the App uses **Google AdMob** (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and share certain device and usage data to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud, including: - **Device or other IDs:** Android Advertising ID and App Set ID - **Approximate location:** General location estimated from your IP address - **App interactions:** App launches, taps, and ad views - **Diagnostics:** App performance, crash/hang rates, and SDK diagnostic metrics All data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. For more information on how Google uses data when you use partner apps, please review: - [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) - [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) ## 4. User Consent & Ad Controls (GDPR / EEA / UK) If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent regarding personalized advertising. Regardless of your location, you can opt out of personalized advertising or reset/delete your Advertising ID at any time through your Android device settings: **Settings > Google > Ads** (or **Settings > Privacy > Ads**). ## 5. Children's Privacy Reverse Play is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through a third-party service in the App, please contact us so we can address it promptly. ## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if we add new features or third-party integrations.
