Privacy Policy for Reverse Play: Voice Challenge

Effective Date: November 21, 2026
Developer: Saad Lakhtyari
Contact: slakhtyari@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Reverse Play: Voice Challenge (package name: com.lakhtyari.reverseplay), developed by Saad Lakhtyari, handles user data, microphone permissions, and third-party advertising services.

1. Microphone Audio & Local Storage

Reverse Play: Voice Challenge requests access to your device's microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) solely to record your voice when you tap the record button and play it back in reverse.

  • 100% Local Processing: All audio recording, reversal, and playback happen strictly in your device's local memory. Your voice recordings are never uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external servers operated by the developer.
  • Optional WAV Export: When you choose to export a reversed clip, the app saves the audio file directly to your device's local storage (such as your Music or Recordings folder) using Android's MediaStore API. You retain full control over these files and can delete them at any time.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

This app displays banner and interstitial advertisements served by Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC). While the developer does not collect personal account information directly, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares certain device and interaction data with Google and its ad technology partners for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention:

  • Device and Other Identifiers: Android Advertising ID (AAID) and App Set ID.
  • Approximate Location: General geographic location inferred from your device's IP address.
  • App Interactions: Ad impressions, taps, app launches, and interaction signals used to serve ads, cap frequency, and measure ad performance.
  • Diagnostics: Crash logs, app launch time, hang rate, and SDK performance data.

All data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS). To learn more about how Google handles data in partner apps, visit How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. User Consent and Privacy Choices (GDPR / UMP)

Users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a consent form via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK on first launch to manage their preferences for personalized or non-personalized advertising.

  • In-App Privacy Options: You can update or withdraw your consent at any time via the Privacy Settings option inside the app.
  • Device-Level Ad Controls: You can also opt out of personalized ads or reset your Android Advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads menu.

4. Data Retention and International Transfers

Because the developer does not operate backend servers for this app, no user recordings or personal profiles are retained by the developer. Data processed by Google AdMob is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and may be processed on servers located outside your country of residence in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

5. Children's Privacy

Reverse Play: Voice Challenge is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in app features, third-party SDKs, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
